How the opening heuristic works

A location's production is the sum of the probability weights of its adjacent number tokens, where each number has 1–5 pips. Two-settlement openings add production, resource diversity, and strategy-specific production weights for wood–brick or ore–wheat–sheep. A small number-diversity adjustment discourages concentration on a single roll. The displayed score is normalized across eligible options on the selected board; it is not calibrated against observed game outcomes.

Expected cards from starting settlements over N rolls = N × (sum of adjoining token pips) / 36

This formula is valid only for the static dice-only experiment; duplicate number exposures count independently per settlement. Robber effects, resource limits, development, and trading are intentionally excluded.

We won't confuse correlation, simulation, and causality.

Our roadmap is to validate the human-game feature schema, reproduce aggregates independently, benchmark against matched simulated games, then calibrate win models on held-out outcomes. The site deliberately makes incomplete experiments visible rather than filling them with invented results.
